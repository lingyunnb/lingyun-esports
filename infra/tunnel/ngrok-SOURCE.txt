WHY NGROK (decision 2026-10-04)
  The owner has NO domain and needs a FIXED public address at zero cost.
  Cloudflare Quick Tunnel gives a RANDOM trycloudflare.com subdomain on every
  restart. SakuraFrp was abandoned: its TCP nodes intercept plaintext HTTP, and
  TLS there needs a domain or a self-signed cert plus a patched client.
  The ngrok FREE plan includes ONE permanent static domain (xxxx.ngrok-free.app)
  with HTTPS and WebSocket support, so no domain purchase is required.

FLAG COMPATIBILITY (probed on this build, 2026-10-04)
  `ngrok http <port> --domain=<host>`  still works but logs:
      "Flag --domain has been deprecated, use --url instead"
  Our scripts therefore use the forward-compatible form:
      ngrok http 3100 --url https://<domain> --log=stdout
  Both forms were confirmed to be accepted; only authentication is required to
  actually open a session (ERR_NGROK_4018 until an authtoken is registered).
